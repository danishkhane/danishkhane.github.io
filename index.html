About

GoHighLevel

1.5+ years building inside GoHighLevel

Most businesses run GoHighLevel at maybe 20% of what it can actually do — leads sit in a default pipeline nobody customized, forms collect names but never qualify anyone, and the calendar still gets double-booked. I build the other 80%: the automations, structure, and logic that make GHL run the business instead of the business running GHL.

01

Calendars & Booking

The problem is usually double-bookings, no-shows, and staff manually confirming every appointment by text. I build multi-staff calendars with automated confirmations, reminders, and reschedule logic — so the calendar runs itself and no-show rates actually drop.

02

Workflow Automations

Leads go cold when follow-up depends on someone remembering to do it. I build automations that move leads through the pipeline, trigger the right follow-up at the right time, and route opportunities to the right person — automatically, every time, with nothing depending on memory.

03

Funnels & Landing Pages

A funnel that isn't tested is a guess. I build A/B split-tested, conversion-optimized funnels with custom domain mapping, so you're not just capturing traffic — you know which version actually converts, and why.

04

Pipelines & Opportunities

Generic pipeline templates hide where deals are actually getting stuck. I structure stages around each client's real sales process, so every opportunity has a clear next step and nothing quietly dies in a stage nobody's watching.

05

Forms & Surveys

Unqualified leads waste a sales team's time before anyone even picks up the phone. I build forms and surveys that score and tag leads the moment they come in, so your team knows who's worth calling first.

06

White-Labeling & Snapshots

Agencies reselling GHL under their own name can't have "Powered by GoHighLevel" showing up in front of clients. I set up white-labeled sub-accounts and reusable snapshots, so every new client gets a fully branded system deployed in minutes, not rebuilt from scratch.
